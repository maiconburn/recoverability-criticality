## 2. Application map (ranked by concreteness)

### Direct

**1. EP / non-Hermitian sensor debate (photonics, optomechanics, quantum)**
- *Today:* an active and unresolved debate: Loughlin-Sudhir PRL 132, 243601 (2024) ("no fundamental SNR gain"), 2024-26 counter-currents (EP accelerometers, Rydberg electrometers 2506.12861, QFI bounds 2603.10614, off-EP biasing 2606.04386). The open question is exactly "for which task and which noise does the EP help or hurt".
- *How our result fits in:* C1 injects a third quantity absent from the debate (inverse-problem cost, intrinsic, noise-independent: the convergence rate halves at the EP even without noise); C4 is the experimental arbitration: the answer is task-dependent: the EP taxes estimation, not recovery. The fit-free alpha recipe is testable on any binary EP sensor.
- *Who to show it to:* Vivishek Sudhir (MIT), Clerk (Chicago), Lau (SFU), Vahala (Caltech), Lan Yang (WUSTL), Wiersig (Magdeburg), Khajavikhan/Christodoulides (USC).

**2. LVK O4 ringdown pipelines / black hole spectroscopy**
- *Today:* GW250114 with a confident measurement of two QNMs including the (2,2,1) overtone; reanalyses raising significances (2605.03576); the GW150914 overtone controversy persists, sensitive to start-time and priors.
- *How our result fits in:* C3 is measured on the same SXS catalog that validates these pipelines and turns folklore ("overtones are hard") into a quantitative conditioning law: a gap-dependent prior for model selection; spin bands where posteriors are structurally ill-conditioned (a non-detection there is not evidence against Kerr). Honest caveat to state: current detections are n=0/1, not n=5/6, and C3 is in noise-free NR fits; the law needs a demonstration at the low-n crossings and with injected detector noise.
- *Who to show it to:* the LVK Testing-GR group; maintainers of pyRing (Carullo), ringdown (Isi-Farr), rational filter (Sizheng Ma / Yanbei Chen's group); the Berti group (Johns Hopkins).

**3. Non-Hermitian spectral theory of Kerr QNMs (avoided crossings, EPs, pseudospectra)**
- *Today:* a 2024-26 surge: resonant excitation at avoided crossings near EPs (PRL 134.141401), pole-skipping connected to the n=5/6 structure (2605.17840), the pseudospectrum program (Jaramillo, Destounis).
- *How our result fits in:* the community has characterized the spectrum, not the inference cost of living near it; C2 and C3 supply that half: a measured error law + the q^2_c(lambda) trajectory and extinction at 0.1091(2), independently verifiable. They are the natural arbiters and first citers.
- *Who to show it to:* Motohashi and Takahashi; Zhu/Yang/Chen (Caltech); Jaramillo/Destounis; Grozdanov and Blake (holographic pole-skipping).

**4. Characterization/calibration of superconducting devices near Liouvillian degeneracies and TLS crossings**
- *Today:* Liouvillian EPs are routine experimental objects (Nat. Commun. 2025/2026); TLS drift is the dominant calibration pain (Google adaptive spectroscopy 2608.02086, TLS tuning 2503.04702, CaliQEC). Qubit-TLS resonance is an avoided crossing with an EP at a complex parameter, the same geometry as C3.
- *How our result fits in:* sigma ~ gap^{-1.11} is a shot/time budget law for resolving two hybridizing modes; C4 says spectral fitting near a Liouvillian EP needs ~4x the budget, but operational tasks (recovery/control) are NOT penalized: an actionable decoupling for calibration policy: EP-grade characterization is not needed to operate.
- *Who to show it to:* the Google Quantum AI calibration team (Klimov), IBM's device-quality group, Kater Murch (WUSTL), the KIT/Ustinov TLS groups, the Kerr-cat teams (AWS/Yale/ENS).

**5. Petz-recovery-based error correction/mitigation**
- *Today:* a growing builder community: the Mandayam group (IIT Madras) with noise-adapted QEC break-even on IBM hardware (PRR 6, 043034), 2025 NMR/ion-trap/optics implementations, Q-NEXT publicizing the direction.
- *How our result fits in:* C4 is literally a Petz circuit on a cloud QPU; the protocol message is that Petz recovery does not inherit the ill-conditioning of channel identification near spectral degeneracy: noise-adapted recovery can be built from coarse estimates exactly where full tomography pays the 4x tax (relevant vs. PEC, which needs the learned channel).
- *Who to show it to:* Prabha Mandayam and coauthors, the NMR/ion-trap Petz teams, Q-NEXT QEC researchers, IBM's error mitigation group.

**6. Quantum system identification / Hamiltonian and Lindbladian learning**
- *Today:* the 2025-26 wave of learning-based characterization (iPINN-HL, meta-learning for superconducting qubits, QNNs); no framework carries an a priori complexity statement in degeneracy-limited regimes.
- *How our result fits in:* C1 provides the missing a priori law: a stopping criterion / sample budget formula near degeneracies; a falsifiable diagnostic (plot residuals in the collapse variable to distinguish EP-limited from noise-limited); a warning that loss landscapes of Lindbladian fits flatten with a universal exponent at Liouvillian EPs.
- *Who to show it to:* the iPINN-HL and Hamiltonian meta-learning authors; Lindbladian learning theorists; Sandia's QCVV community (pyGSTi).

**7. Super-resolution mathematics / Prony-ESPRIT conditioning**
- *Today:* a mature, cited program: the Vandermonde condition number with nearly-colliding nodes scales as SRF^{2p-1} (Batenkov-Demanet-Goldman-Yomdin), the ESPRIT bound, follow-ups through 2025.
- *How our result fits in:* C1 is the same mathematical phenomenon (confluent node = EP of the companion operator) in a form this community has not stated: exponential-in-N with the rate exactly halved at confluence, parameter-free collapse, alpha predicted without fitting. It is the right audience to generalize/formalize it, and the source of the most dangerous referee objection ("known since 2020"), which the README must preempt by explicitly reconciling gap^{-(2p-1)} at fixed N vs. e^{-alpha N}/sqrt(d).
- *Who to show it to:* Batenkov (Tel Aviv), Demanet (MIT), Fannjiang (UC Davis), Wenjing Liao (Georgia Tech).

## 3. Final verdict

**Adjust the phrasing, then publish. No claim needs to be withheld.**

Grounds:

1. **None of the four claims is entirely new, and none is entirely known.** All are "partially known" with a real defensible core: the synthesis into a predictive law (C1), the EP map + extinction in scalar EGB at real momentum (C2), the empirical scaling law in real NR (C3), the dual-task design (C4). All four cores survive the August 2026 prior-art search.

2. **The dominant risk is over-claiming ingredients, not lack of substance.** The adjustments in the table above are mandatory before publishing; the most critical are: (a) C1: reconcile explicitly with Batenkov et al. (algebraic at fixed N vs. exponential in N) and derive the Stahl collapse instead of presenting it as a discovery; (b) C2: replace "strictly above the causality bound" with the neutral numerical formulation and define "extinguished" operationally, relating it to the Konoplya-Zhidenko 1/8 threshold; (c) C3: cite and discuss the exponent difference with Imafuku-Oshita-Takeda (-1.11 vs. -2) and check a_c = 0.8975 vs. 0.90; (d) C4: downgrade each ingredient to cited prior art and frame the Murch result as a reanalysis of public data.

3. **Three verification items pending before submission (they do not block the repository, they block the paper):** (i) C4: a final search pass on "pretty good measurement + EP" and pseudo-Hermitian NV experiments from Chinese groups (Duan-lab, QFI-at-EP, PRL 2020-21), since the session's search was quota-limited; check that no backflow reanalysis of the Murch dataset already reports an equivalent quantity; a courtesy notification to the Murch group. (ii) C2: confirm numerically whether the extinction is migration to complex q^2 or genuine disappearance, and that it does not coincide with the non-perturbative imaginary branch. (iii) C3: state the n=5/6 vs. n=0/1 caveat and the absence of detector noise in the README, so as not to invite a reading of immediate applicability to LVK pipelines.

4. **No hype:** avoid "unprecedented", "first in history", and any implication of a mechanism between EP extinction and causality (C2) without a derivation. The repository's strength is laptop reproducibility + predictions without fit parameters + validation on virgin data; that stands on its own with the calibrated phrasing above.
