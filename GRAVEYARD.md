# The graveyard — what we discarded, with pride

Every entry below is a hypothesis we took seriously, tested, and killed —
most of them our own, several within hours of proposing them. The trail is
public because the discipline that kills wrong ideas before readers see
them survive IS the method. Detailed post-mortems live in `ERRATA.md` and
the linked reports.

| "Information is a single currency" (spectral cost ⇔ recovery cost locking) | Refuted three times over: in simulation, in published superconducting-qubit data, and on a real quantum processor. | `prelab/REPORT_QPU.md`, `p6/RESULTS_P6.md` |
| P10.1: a Hermitian periodic drive creates exceptional points at the Floquet replica resonance | Died by its own frozen criterion: the resonance opens a Rabi-type anticrossing (gap growing with drive amplitude, eigenvectors far from collinear). The death taught the mechanism: Hermitian coupling between frequency layers opens gaps; dissipative coupling makes them collide, which became the sharpened P10.4 and was confirmed at the 1e-10 level. | `results/RESULTS_P10_FLOQUET.md` |
| P11.1: a wormhole's twin-throat doublet splits exponentially in the throat separation | Died of a modeling error made at freezing time: the exponential symmetric-antisymmetric doublet belongs to a BOUND double well; an open two-barrier cavity (the wormhole) instead carries a tower of cavity modes (the echoes) whose spacing scales like pi over the cavity length, which is what the validated instrument measured. The correct detectability question (small cavity-mode amplitudes under the barrier ringdown) is posed but not yet frozen. | `results/RESULTS_P11_WORMHOLE.md` |
| P12.1: the minimal coupled-qubit wormhole (3+3, no decoder) shows a traversability window | Dead with a physics lesson, after two instrument iterations (a misplaced message caught by a sanity check, then a broken qubit-embedding caught by unit tests): with the clean protocol the coupling alone transfers at most 0.02 bits to the mirror qubit. The decoder-free window is a large-N semiclassical phenomenon; small systems need the Yoshida-Kitaev decoder, which is the documented next step. | `results/RESULTS_P12_P13.md` |
| P13.1 (amplitude task): the degenerating subring amplitude costs gap^-3 | The isolated-pair exponent does not survive the multi-tone tower: the measured exponent is a stable -2.40 over two decades, the cluster regime of Prony-type estimation. Scope refinement recorded; the frequency task confirmed its window. | `results/RESULTS_P12_P13.md` |
| P14.1: cloud-coupling estimation at a boson-cloud level crossing costs gap^-2 | The frozen exponent belonged to the spectral-splitting task; the Landau-Zener population record is an amplitude-like task and costs gap^-0.9. One more entry for the lesson that the hierarchy is per task, not per system. (The v1 instrument also hid a singular Fisher behind a pseudo-inverse, caught because a zero Cramer-Rao bound is impossible.) | `results/RESULTS_P14_GRAVCOLLIDER.md` |
| P12''.2 (original form): the wormhole decoder tracks the Petz recovery of the UNCONDITIONED channel | Forbidden by no-signaling, and the data enforced the theorem spontaneously: the unconditioned Petz fidelity sat at exactly 0.2500 regardless of scrambling depth. Reformulated for the conditioned channel and then confirmed at correlation 0.979. A prediction killed by a theorem the numbers volunteered. | `results/RESULTS_P12_P13.md` |
| P8-F2: the EP log survives the slow-roll sweep in the free-field two-scale spectrum | Killed twice with a diagnosis each time: an integer-exponent basis that turned the log into a spurious compensator (the no-crossing control lit up instead of the crossing), then a mode-referenced residual test showing only smooth adiabatic ln and ln-squared corrections proportional to the sweep rate, identical with and without the crossing. The free sector is excluded as a carrier; the dynamical EP signature, if it exists, lives only in the interacting in-in correlator. | `results/RESULTS_P8_PHASE1.md` adendo 9 |

| P15.4: the cost anisotropy along an EP-3 line is set by the local Petermann factor | Killed by its own frozen scale control on real trapped-ion data: the eigenvector condition number anti-correlates with the measured cost (-0.27, wrong sign) while the bare damping rate correlates better (0.525), flagging the mundane confounder the freeze anticipated - stronger damping means fewer observable oscillations in a fixed window. The geometric second law, if any, needs raw records rather than aggregated bootstraps. | `results/RESULTS_P15_EPN.md` |
| P16.1: the Jordan secular channel t*exp(-gt) is chi2-detectable in time-domain probe decoherence at an EP | Killed by its own frozen criterion with a clean instrument: two free damped exponentials mimic the secular channel to noise level 1e-3 over a 4/gamma window (Prony-type identifiability), chi2/dof ~ 1 for both models even exactly at the EP. Instructive contrast with P8.2, where the log channel in a scale variable is detectable at chi2/dof up to 1e5: Jordan-channel detectability depends on the observable class. | `results/RESULTS_P16_DECOHERENCE.md` |
| P16.2: decoherence-based estimation of the control parameter costs gap^-1 near the EP | Killed: sigma(dq2) is flat over 1.5 decades of gap (p = -0.07 vs frozen [0.6, 1.5]). The sqrt-unfolding response gain cancels the spectral resolution cost exactly - third independent sighting of the pattern (P14-LZ, LEP3 design, here): control-parameter estimation is EP-neutral; only spectral parameters pay the critical cost. Candidate general statement. | `results/RESULTS_P16_DECOHERENCE.md` |
| Applied line: a resolvability-gated sensor-management reward (schedule the expensive sensor only in the information-collapse window) beats a flat usage penalty in Stone Soup | Killed by its own two frozen benchmarks on the framework's tutorial topology: identical label-swap rate and RMSE to a physics-blind flat penalty in both crossing (v1) and sustained-formation (v2) scenarios, because brief crossings create too little ambiguity and parallel formations create none. The 81-84% duty-cycle cut over the stock uncertainty reward is real but belongs to trivial cost awareness, not to our theorem. No PR submitted; ablation baselines caught the misattribution before it went out. Freeze and data: ~/fisica/contrib/stonesoup_bench_FROZEN.md, ss_bench*.log. | external benchmark (contrib) |
| P20.2 M2: direct time-resolved intensity detection of an eigenmode-incoherent EP pair pays the gap curse (0 / -1) | Void by a modeling oversight visible after the fact: a real unfolding gives the two eigenfrequencies identical decay rates, and an incoherent sum has no interference term, so the intensity is independent of epsilon at every order and the Fisher information is identically zero (measured sigma ~ 1e28, roundoff slope ~ 1). All the information sits in the mode structure; the counting and QFI predictions of the same cycle were confirmed at four digits. Lesson: check that the observable depends on the parameter before predicting how much it costs. | `results/RESULTS_P20_RAYLEIGH_EP.md` |
| P8-F4'.1: the F3 localization control fails only because its basis was wrong; with the nu-correct basis the log improvement disappears away from the degeneracy | Killed by the regenerated gate data: with {x^{1/2-nu}, x^{1/2+nu}, x^{1/2+nu} ln x} at nu = 0.85 the log term still improves the fit 21x (R(1) = 10.7, ratio 0.5 against the frozen > 7). A third basis function absorbs higher tower terms wherever it sits; the improvement is generic slack. Companion F4'.2 confirmed that at nu = 1 two nearby powers reproduce and beat the log basis, so the Jordan log is a confluent limit, not a channel; the day-of-compute survival run was cancelled on this basis. | `results/RESULTS_P8_PHASE1.md` |
| P21d.1: the number of counter-rotating modes that escape absorption by the branch cut equals |m| | Killed at m = -3: only n = 0 and n = 1 escape (collapse onto the origin as 1/B); n = 2 arrives at the cut at B_c ~ 1.64 (linear approach, deep continued-fraction probe at kmax 10k/20k after the kmax-600 tracker had produced a truncation-artifact trajectory hugging the cut). Escape counts 1, 2, 2 for |m| = 1, 2, 3; the m = -4 run (P21e) decides between saturation at two and floor((|m|+2)/2). | `results/RESULTS_P21_VORTEX.md` |
| P21e rule (a): the number of escaping counter-rotating modes saturates at two for every |m| >= 2 | Killed at m = -4: n = 0, 1, 2 all escape (collapse onto the origin as 1/B, kmax-independent), n = 3 arrives at B_c = 1.05. Counts 1, 2, 2, 3 for |m| = 1..4 follow floor((|m|+2)/2), the competing rule frozen alongside; a WKB derivation of the rule and of the collapse coefficients c_n(m) is the open follow-up. | `results/RESULTS_P21_VORTEX.md` |
| P21f.2: the pure-vortex resonance count (Re c > 0, Im c > -2) equals floor((|m|+2)/2) and predicts 3 escaping modes at |m| = 5 | Killed at |m| = 5: the scaled problem has four such resonances (each a stable minimum of the Wronskian landscape), one more than the finite-B floor rule; whether the extra one is a fourth escaping B = 0 overtone or a mode born from the branch cut is the P21g question. Confirmed alongside: the scaled eigenvalues reproduce all eight finite-B limits within 2% (P21f.1). | `results/RESULTS_P21_VORTEX.md` |
| P21f.3: first-order Schutz-Will WKB on the scaled pure-vortex potential reproduces the tower within 15-20% at |m| >= 3 | Killed: the fundamental is within 8-15% at |m| = 4 but the overtone spacing is not the light-ring Lyapunov exponent at these azimuthal numbers (m = -4, n = 1: WKB 1.32-0.36i against 0.79-0.73i); the eikonal limit needs much larger |m|. The exact scaled solver stays the instrument. | `results/RESULTS_P21_VORTEX.md` |
