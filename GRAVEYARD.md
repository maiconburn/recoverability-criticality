# The graveyard — what we discarded, with pride

Every entry below is a hypothesis we took seriously, tested, and killed —
most of them our own, several within hours of proposing them. The trail is
public because the discipline that kills wrong ideas before readers see
them survive IS the method. Detailed post-mortems live in `ERRATA.md` and
the linked reports.

| P12''.2 (original form): the wormhole decoder tracks the Petz recovery of the UNCONDITIONED channel | Forbidden by no-signaling, and the data enforced the theorem spontaneously: the unconditioned Petz fidelity sat at exactly 0.2500 regardless of scrambling depth. | P8-F2: the EP log survives the slow-roll sweep in the free-field two-scale spectrum | Killed twice with a diagnosis each time: an integer-exponent basis that turned the log into a spurious compensator (the no-crossing control lit up instead of the crossing), then a mode-referenced residual test showing only smooth adiabatic ln and ln-squared corrections proportional to the sweep rate, identical with and without the crossing. | P20.2 M2: direct time-resolved intensity detection of an eigenmode-incoherent EP pair pays the gap curse (0 / -1) | Void by a modeling oversight visible after the fact: a real unfolding gives the two eigenfrequencies identical decay rates, and an incoherent sum has no interference term, so the intensity is independent of epsilon at every order and the Fisher information is identically zero (measured sigma ~ 1e28, roundoff slope ~ 1).
